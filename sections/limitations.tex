\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
The results in \cref{sec:results} are valid within the pre-registered protocol (\cref{sec:evaluation}). This section points out where the limits of this protocol are. There are two types of limitations. First, limitations in the design, and second, in the evidence, with the second being a limitation in the data. The most serious limitation concerns the best performing model family. All limitations pointed out here do not change the ranking at all; they just put it into context.

\subsection{Pretraining contamination of the zero-shot models}
\label{sec:lim:contamination}
%------------------------------------------------------------------------
% WARUM DAS ZUERST KOMMT: die Kernaussage der Arbeit ist, dass ein
% Zero-Shot-Foundation-Model die besten Intervalle liefert
% (\cref{sec:res:ranking}). Genau dieses Modell ist das einzige, dessen
% Trainingsdaten du nicht kennst. Wenn diese Limitation greift, ist
% Befund (3) nicht abgeschwaecht, sondern hinfaellig. Deshalb Absatz 1.
%
The best performing model family, TiRex-2, was released on 2026-07-01 with no known pretraining cutoff date. Our evaluation window ends before the release date (2015-10 to 2026-04) and by that is potentially part of the training set. This caveat is already documented in \cref{sec:models:tirex2}.
% WAS HIER STEHT:
%  1. Der Sachverhalt nuechtern: TiRex-2 wurde am 2026-07-01 released; der
%     Pretraining-Cutoff ist nicht dokumentiert. Das Evaluationsfenster
%     laeuft 2015-10 bis 2026-04, endet also VOR dem Release, liegt aber
%     potenziell vollstaendig im Pretraining-Zeitraum.
%     -> Verweis \cref{sec:models:tirex2} (dort steht der Caveat schon in
%        zwei Saetzen, Zeilen 236-238). Achtung Defekt [D2].
This is especially relevant for government credit spreads since the time series are publicly accessible, long term and included in a lot of big time series corpora. It does not need an exotic dataset to be included in the time series since credit spreads are part of the standard sources.
%  2. Warum das bei Staatsanleihen-Spreads besonders heikel ist: die
%     zugrundeliegenden Renditereihen sind oeffentlich, langjaehrig und
%     in nahezu jedem grossen Zeitreihen-Korpus enthalten. Es braucht
%     also keinen exotischen Datensatz, damit Kontamination plausibel ist
%     -- die Standardquellen genuegen.
The arguments work against this thesis. First, TiRex-2 is the best model at the point forecast (MAE-Ratio of 0.9965) but it does not statistically beat the Random Walk as can be seen in the Panel-t of -0.4979 and a smallest q-value of 0.3803. A model that remembers the target time series would perform better. The tests proved to have power at the arma/L family (Panel-t +2.4473, FI with q 0.0040).

The standard approach for the pairwise Diebold-Mariano tests at a one step forecasting window (h=1) is to use the sample variance (HAC-Lag 0). A positive autocorrelation in the loss differentials is created by the 12 month refits (block dependency) as well as the state-based adaptive methods. This leads to a systematic underestimation of the sample variance and in turn to overly optimistic country p values. The test results should therefore be seen as an upper bound for the real statistical significance.

%   lehrbuchkonform: bei Prognosehorizont h sind die Fehlerdifferentiale
%   mechanisch bis Lag h-1 autokorreliert, und bei h=1 ist h-1=0.
%   In diesem Design ist der Horizont aber NICHT die einzige Quelle
%   serieller Korrelation:

%          teilen denselben Parametervektor, ihre Fehler sind daher
%          innerhalb des Blocks korreliert (siehe tab:refit_blocks).
%     (ii) Die adaptiven CP-Methoden tragen Zustand ueber die Zeit

To correct for this systematic underestimation of the variance and serial autocorrelation we use a time clustered t Panel test for our main findings. In the first step we calculate the scale free normed loss differentials $\frac{d_{i,t}}{W^{ref}_{i}}$ every month meaned for all 10 countries. This condenses joint cross-sectional effects to a single monthly observation point (N=127). In the second step we calculate the t value of this timeseries mean by using the Newey-West long-term variance with Bartlett-Kernel and a Lag-Window of 3:\\
  
$\sigma^2_{\text{LR}} = \gamma_0 + 2 \sum_{l=1}^{3} \left(1 - \frac{l}{4}\right) \gamma_l$\\

This is a pragmatic one-dimensional version of the Driscoll-Kraay logic. This procedure is not a full two-dimensional Driscoll-Kraay covariance matrix estimator but still controls autocorrelation and cross-sectional correlation sufficiently.

\subsection{Seed fragility of the neural results}

A symmetric score, by structure, cannot correct for this distribution.
